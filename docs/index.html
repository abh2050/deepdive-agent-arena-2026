01 / Method

Trust is a pipeline,
not a prompt.

A LangGraph coordinator assigns distinct jobs, tracks coverage, and loops back when evidence is thin. The final report is assembled by deterministic code, so synthesis cannot add new facts.

01

Decompose

The planner creates focused, non-overlapping subquestions that make gaps visible.

02

Retrieve

Researchers fetch real pages and preserve excerpts, URLs, titles, and quality signals.

03

Challenge

Every atomic claim must pass verbatim quote membership and independent entailment review.

04

Publish

Only verified claims reach the cited report; every rejection remains in the audit ledger.
