# Brag Plan: DeepDive

## What is this app?

DeepDive is a multi-agent research analyst that admits a claim into its report
only after the claim is tied to captured source text and passes two verification
gates.

## The angle

Treat the demo as a compact forensic investigation. The villain is the
convincing-but-unsupported AI sentence; the payoff is the moment a judge opens
the Evidence Inspector and sees the exact quote and verdict. The film proves the
product by using it, rather than stacking feature claims.

## Hook

Cold-open on a verified claim and its evidence drawer with the line: “A citation
is not proof.”

## Key moments

- A real research question enters the live Vercel application.
- The completed run visibly keeps both verified and rejected candidates.
- A citation opens into URL → stored excerpt → exact quote → verdict.
- The audit trail exposes the decisions behind the final report.
- A grounded follow-up returns the evidence IDs it used.

## Outro

“No quote. No verification. No published claim.” End on the live URL.

## User flow worth showing

Enter an open research question → run the multi-agent verification workflow →
inspect the report, evidence, audit trail, and grounded follow-up.

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: forensic evidence thriller
- Interpretation: confident, restrained, and evidence-first; readable UI holds
  matter more than rapid motion.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 116 seconds

The two-minute hackathon walkthrough requested by the user intentionally
overrides Brag’s usual 15–25 second social-video duration.

## Visual identity

- Background: `#080b11`
- Accent: `#06b6d4`
- Text: `#f8fafc`
- Display font: Newsreader
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: Evidence Inspector with highlighted verbatim quote
  and verification verdict

## Share copy

Most AI research asks you to trust the answer. DeepDive makes every published
claim prove itself—down to the stored quote, verifier verdict, and audit event.

## Audio direction

- Role: restrained cinematic support under spoken narration
- Music: low investigative pulse, never triumphant or distracting
- Music treatment: 12–15% under voice; small lift on result reveal; fade under
  the final line
- Audio-reactive treatment: none for the screen recording
- SFX posture: sparse—submit click, verified reveal, final mark only
- Restraint rule: never score every UI movement and never mask speech

## Storyboard

1. **The doubt — 8s:** Evidence Inspector cold open; “A citation is not proof.”
2. **The promise — 11s:** Hero and one-sentence product definition.
3. **The mechanism — 23s:** Briefly open the separate system-design site for
   the verification loop and dual gate, then return to the product workspace.
4. **The live run — 15s:** Submit the heat-pump question; honestly condense wait.
5. **The result — 16s:** Verified/rejected counts, coverage, deterministic report.
6. **The proof — 17s:** Inspect URL, excerpt, exact quote, quality, and verdict.
7. **The record — 12s:** Audit events from plan through completion.
8. **The continuity — 9s:** Grounded follow-up with cited evidence IDs.
9. **The rule — 5s:** Final principle, product name, and live URL.
